How a day works

Five tabs, a full chart page and a learning path with Xiaoqi. Plain words first; the traditional terms are one tap away.

明天 Tomorrow

Tomorrow's energy, each evening

BaZi gives a 0 to 100 score for how tomorrow sits with your chart. Two numbers side by side: the day on its own, and the day within this year (adding the year and your ten-year luck period). Tap “why” for the four pillars as colour blocks with their pluses and minuses, and a two-line sum.

Example

Tomorrow · Fri 20 Nov

The day on its own75High
Within this year65Good

The faded part is the 10 points this year and luck period take off

Strong and steady. A day for the things that matter.

明天 Tomorrow

Twelve two-hour periods, coloured

A Qimen Dunjia chart for each two-hour period, turned into three colours: good, neutral, avoid. The current period is marked “now”; after 23:00 the 子 period belongs to tomorrow's chart, so “now” shows on tomorrow's dial. Tap any period to read why, or open its full chart.

Example

Tomorrow's twelve periodsGoodNeutralAvoid

申 15–17 Open door (開門): good. Horse star (馬星): things move fast

排盤 Chart

The full nine-palace chart

The nine palaces in the traditional layout, south at the top. Colours follow the eight doors, and you and the people you pick are marked. Tap a palace for its stems, star, door, spirit and markers such as void (空亡) or horse star (馬星), each with a plain sentence. Today and tomorrow are free; other dates are Pro.

問事 Ask

Ask now, or pick a time

Each matter is a folder that keeps what you asked, the times you picked and how it went. “Ask now” casts the chart for the moment you tap, and the answer can open the full chart for that moment. Or give a date range and hours and get the three best slots and two to avoid. Asking the same thing again in the same period gives the same answer.

人 People

You and them

Add people with a full birth date, only the year, or none. Without a year, tag them by role (senior, boss, peer, junior, partner) and Chengshi reads them the traditional way. Each person gets a weekly heatmap of who has the upper hand in each period. Tap a cell for that period's full chart.

Example
  • KaiPeer · friendHelps you
  • ManagerDirect boss · year onlyEven
  • New clientClient · no birth dateLeans on you

This is fixed by birth and does not change with time; the best hours to talk change every week.

地 Places

Saved places and directions

Save the places you go. Chengshi works out their direction and distance from your starting point, then checks that direction on the chart for the hour you leave. It tells you when a place sits close to the edge between two directions.

對帳 Check-ins

Check it against your days

If you like, take a few seconds each day to note how it actually went. A progress bar shows when the next conclusion comes: at 14, 30 and 60 check-ins, then every 30. Each conclusion compares the match with a chance baseline and shows which kinds of days fit best. With enough evidence it can suggest other favourable elements; nothing changes until you tap Apply. Free shows the headline and overall match; the rest is Pro.

How it works, in three layers

Building the chart, scoring it and checking it against your days are three separate things.

  1. 1

    The chart: checked chart by chart

    Pillars, solar terms and every Qimen chart were checked one by one against the original program and a public calendar library: over 220,000 charts and 20,000 pillar moments, all matching.

  2. 2

    The score: Chengshi's method

    Energy follows the BaZi support-and-drain reading. Hours use a rotating Qimen Dunjia chart, with the eight doors rated by their traditional classes. Chengshi's scoring method (version 6) turns these into points, and the app shows every item.

  3. 3

    The check-in: your own records

    Spend a few seconds each day noting how it went. The check-in page charts Chengshi's suggestions next to what you reported, against a chance baseline, with a conclusion at 14, 30 and 60 check-ins. Conclusions are worked out on the phone.
