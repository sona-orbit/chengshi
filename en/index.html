How a day works

Five tabs and a learning path with Xiaoqi. Plain words first; the traditional terms are one tap away.

明天 Tomorrow

Tomorrow's energy, each evening

BaZi day pillars give a 0 to 100 score for how tomorrow sits with your chart. Two numbers side by side: the day on its own (the day and the month), and the day within this year (adding the year and your ten-year luck period). Tap “why” to see every part of the sum.

Example

Tomorrow · Fri 20 Nov

The day on its own75High
Within this year65Good

The faded part is the 10 points this year and luck period take off

Strong and steady. A day for the things that matter.

明天 Tomorrow

Twelve two-hour periods, coloured

A Qimen Dunjia chart for each two-hour period, turned into three colours: good, neutral, avoid. The current period is marked; tap any one to read why.

Example

Tomorrow's twelve periodsGoodNeutralAvoid

申 15–17 Open door (開門): good. Horse star (馬星): things move fast

Tap a period

問事 Ask

Ask now, or pick a time

Each matter is a folder that keeps what you asked, the times you picked and how it went. Ask about the current period, or give a date range and hours and get the three best slots and two to avoid. Asking the same thing again in the same period gives the same answer.

Example

人 People

You and them

Add people with a full birth date, only the year, or none. Without a birth date, tag them by the traditional roles (elder or senior, direct boss, peer, junior, partner) and Chengshi reads them the traditional way. You can also find the best times this week to talk with them.

Example
  • KaiPeer · friendHelps you
  • ManagerDirect boss · year onlyEven
  • New clientClient · no birth dateLeans on you

This is fixed by birth and does not change with time; the best hours to talk change every week.

地 Places

Saved places and directions

Save the places you go. Chengshi works out their direction and distance from your starting point, then checks that direction on the chart for the hour you leave. It tells you when a place sits close to the edge between two directions.

對帳 Check-ins

Check it against your days

If you like, spend a few seconds each day noting how it actually went. The check-in page charts how often Chengshi matched what you reported, next to what guessing would get. Everything works without check-ins too.

Is it accurate? Three separate answers

“Accurate” means three different things here, and each has a different answer.

  1. 1

    The chart: calculated correctly

    Pillars, solar terms and every Qimen chart were checked one by one against the original program and a public calendar library: over 220,000 charts and 20,000 pillar moments, all matching. This part can be verified, and we did.

  2. 2

    The reading: our own rules

    Schools disagree on how to read a chart. Chengshi's energy and hour scores follow rules we wrote (version 6). You can read them in the app, but they have not been validated.

  3. 3

    Whether it fits you: check for yourself

    There is no reliable evidence that readings like these match a person's life. So Chengshi does not claim accuracy; treat it as a reference for planning and looking back, and use the check-in page to see how it lines up with your own days.
