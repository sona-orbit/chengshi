Privacy policy

Chengshi has no account and no server of ours. What you enter stays on your iPhone. Below is each case where the app connects to the internet, and what it sends.

The app's screens are in Traditional Chinese only for now. More languages will come later.

Data on your phone

Your birth date and time, gender, the people you add, saved places, matter folders, past questions, check-ins, changes to your favourable elements, finished lessons and settings. Check-in conclusions and element suggestions are also worked out on the phone. They are stored in the app's own folder and never reach us. They are included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer, as your phone is set up).

Location

Only the 地 (Places) tab uses it. The app asks for location permission only when you choose your current position as a starting point. Your location is used on the phone to work out directions and is not sent anywhere.

Maps and place search

Searching for a place or viewing the map in 地 (Places) uses Apple's map service (MapKit): your search text is sent to Apple for results, and map tiles come from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

The evening reminder is a local notification scheduled on the phone. No server is involved. You can turn it off in Settings.

AI explanations (optional, off by default)

With “Apple 內建” (Apple on-device), Apple's on-device model runs on the phone and nothing is sent. With “我的 API key” (my API key), when you ask for an AI explanation the phone sends a fact sheet directly to the AI service you chose in Settings, not through us. The fact sheet contains the computed results and reasons (energy, hours, directions and so on), the matter you wrote, and, if you picked a person, their name and the tags you gave them. It contains no one's birth date. Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on this phone. That service handles what it receives under its own terms.

Reporting a problem

Only sent when you open 設定 (Settings) → 回報問題 (Report a problem) and tap send. It contains what you wrote, contact details if you chose to give them, the app version, iOS version, device model, language, and one line of settings (chart method, rule version, whether you changed your favourable elements, how many check-ins). It goes to our own collector (Google Apps Script) and is kept in our Google Sheet, used only to fix problems and reply to you. A random ID, created the first time you send a report, is attached only to limit how many reports can be sent per hour; it is not linked to who you are. Your birth data, people, places, matters and check-ins are never sent.

Purchases

Apple handles Chengshi Pro purchases and subscriptions. We never see your payment details; the app only asks the App Store whether you own Pro.

What the app does not have

No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDK, and we do not share your data with other companies.

Deleting your data

設定 (Settings) → 清除全部資料 (Erase all data) deletes the app's data and cannot be undone. Clear the API key field to delete the key. Deleting the app also deletes its data. To delete a report you sent, send another report asking us to.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website, not to the app. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Contact

For privacy questions, use 設定 (Settings) → 回報問題 (Report a problem) in the app, or email: sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

If this policy changes, this page and the date below will be updated.
