What Chengshi is

Chengshi builds charts from the traditional Chinese calendar (BaZi and Qimen Dunjia) and turns them into scores and suggestions with Chengshi's scoring method, for planning your time and looking back. Chengshi does not promise any outcome.

Not professional advice

Nothing in Chengshi is medical, psychological, legal, financial or investment advice. Make important decisions with your own judgement and ask a professional when you need one.

Chengshi Pro

Pro adds lessons 6 to 17, charts for dates other than today and tomorrow, and the full check-in conclusions and suggestions; every other feature is in the free version. Pro comes as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase, with prices shown on the purchase screen.

Subscriptions and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the next period is charged within 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The one-time purchase is paid once and does not renew.

Restoring and refunds

On a new phone or after reinstalling, use 設定 (Settings) → 乘時 Pro → 恢復購買 (Restore purchases) with the same Apple Account. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

AI explanations are optional. With your own API key, your use of that service is under its terms and billed to your own account. The AI only rewrites results the app has already computed.
