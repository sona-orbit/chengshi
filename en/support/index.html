Support

Common questions are below. If yours is not there, report it in the app; we read every report.

The app follows your iPhone's language and comes in ten languages. The screenshots on this site are from the Traditional Chinese version.

Report a problem

In the app, open Settings → Report a Problem, describe what happened and tap send. Leave a way to reach you (optional) if you want a reply. Only the app version, iOS version, device model, language and a few settings go with it, never your birth data, people, places or matters.

If you have not installed the app yet, or it will not open, email: sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

Questions

How are the scores worked out?

Three layers. The chart: pillars, solar terms and every Qimen chart were checked one by one against the original program and a public calendar library, and they match. The score: energy follows the BaZi support-and-drain reading, hours use a rotating Qimen Dunjia chart with the eight doors rated by their traditional classes, and Chengshi's scoring method (version 6) turns these into points, shown item by item in the app. The check-in: the check-in page shows how well the suggestions match your own records.

Why are there two energy numbers?

“The day on its own” looks only at the day and the month, and changes every day. “Within this year” also adds the year and your ten-year luck period, which stay the same for a whole year or decade and pull every day up or down. Side by side, you can see the day and the year separately. Xiaoqi's face and the tip follow the first number.

Why does 23:00 count as the next day?

Traditionally a day starts at the 子 period, 23:00 to 01:00. Chengshi follows this: from 23:00 the day pillar moves to the next day and the hour dial shows tomorrow's chart. So after 23:00, “now” appears on tomorrow's dial. If you were born between 23:00 and midnight, your day pillar is the next day's too.

Which clock does it use?

Your phone's time zone and clock time; it does not convert to true solar time. Birth times are taken as the local time you enter.

Can I use it without a birth time?

Yes. Turn off “I know my birth time”. Chengshi then leaves out the hour pillar and the luck periods, so “within this year” only adds the year. Everything else works.

Which dates can the chart page show?

Today and tomorrow are free, with every period of both days. Other dates are Pro. Opening it from Tomorrow, an Ask answer, Pick a time or People takes you to that period's chart.

When does a check-in conclusion come?

The first at 14 check-ins, then at 30 and 60, then every 30. The progress bar at the top of the check-in page shows how many days are left. Each conclusion only uses check-ins up to its milestone, so it does not change day to day. It is worked out on the phone and not sent anywhere.

Will an element suggestion change my settings by itself?

No. It changes only when you tap Apply, and you can switch back; check-ins before and after are compared separately. You can set how many check-ins and how big a gap it needs under Settings → Advanced → Suggestion thresholds; the recommended values are 30 check-ins, 0.25 levels, and the chance check on.

What is the difference between free and Pro?

Pro adds lessons 6 to 17, charts for dates other than today and tomorrow, and the full check-in conclusions and suggestions. Free shows each conclusion's headline, overall match and best-matching kind of day; everything else is the same. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase, with prices as shown on the App Store. Pro has no ads; the free version shows one at the bottom of the Tomorrow page.

Does the free version have ads?

One banner, at the very bottom of the Tomorrow page, from Google AdMob. Chengshi asks only for non-personalised ads and shows no tracking prompt. Ask, answers, the chart, lessons and the purchase screen have no ads. Pro (monthly, yearly or one-time) has no ads at all.

Which languages does the app come in?

Ten: Traditional and Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese and Vietnamese. It follows your iPhone's language; to change only Chengshi's, open the iPhone's Settings → Chengshi → Language. On the chart itself the stems, branches, doors and stars stay in Chinese characters, as tradition writes them; tap one for the explanation in your language.

Which chart method should I pick?

The default is 拆補 (chaibu). About a third of periods come out differently under 置閏 (zhirun), and both are in use. If unsure, keep the default; you can change it under Settings → Advanced → Chart method.

How do I report a problem?

Open Settings → Report a Problem, describe it and tap send. The app only connects when you tap send. Leave contact details if you want a reply.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and open Settings → Chengshi Pro → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions and cancel Chengshi Pro, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. The one-time purchase does not renew.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Erase All Data. Your birth data, people, matters, places, check-ins and lessons are deleted and cannot be restored. If you entered an API key for AI explanations, clear the key field and it is removed from the Keychain. Deleting the app also deletes its data. We have no server, so there is no other copy.

What does the AI feature send?

AI is off by default. With Apple's on-device model nothing leaves the phone. With your own API key, the phone sends a fact sheet straight to the service you chose: the computed results and reasons, the matter you wrote, and, if you picked a person, their name and tags. No birth dates. The AI only rewrites the fact sheet in plain words; it cannot change the conclusion.
