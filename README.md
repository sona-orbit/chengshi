# Chengshi (乘時) — a Qimen Dunjia and BaZi timing planner for iPhone

Chengshi is an iPhone app for personal timing and reflection with the traditional Chinese calendar. Enter your birth date once; each evening it shows tomorrow's energy, which of the twelve two-hour periods suit you, and the full nine-palace Qimen Dunjia chart, so you can pick a time before a talk, a trip or a decision. It is for people who want to plan with BaZi and Qimen Dunjia and check the suggestions against their own days.

## Features

- **Tomorrow's energy, each evening**: a 0 to 100 BaZi score for the day on its own and within this year, with a "why" view of the four pillars.
- **Twelve two-hour periods, coloured**: a Qimen Dunjia chart for each period, shown as good, neutral or avoid. Tap any period to read why or open its full chart.
- **The full nine-palace chart**: stems, star, door, spirit and markers such as void (空亡) or horse star (馬星), each explained in a plain sentence.
- **Ask now, or pick a time**: cast a chart for this moment, or give a date range and hours and get the three best slots and two to avoid. Each matter keeps its own folder.
- **You and them**: add people by full birth date, year only, or role, and see a weekly heatmap of who has the upper hand in each period.
- **Saved places and directions**: Chengshi checks the direction of a place on the chart for the hour you leave.
- **Track record**: note how each day went; conclusions at 14, 30 and 60 check-ins compare the match with a chance baseline.
- **Seventeen short lessons** with Xiaoqi, using your real chart. No account, no server of ours, no analytics SDK; AI explanations are optional and off by default.
